# Yogi Quiz 🤔

<img src="icon_preview.png" width="120" align="right" />

**A colourful trivia game.** No ads, no account.

* **🇳🇵 Nepal pack, offline**: history, geography, mountains, festivals, culture, writers and records.
* **General** and **Science** packs, also offline.
* **Thousands more online** from the Open Trivia Database: Mixed, General, Science, History, Geography, Sports, Movies, Music, Computers, Maths, Animals, Mythology, Books and Video games.
* **Difficulty**: Any, Easy, Medium or Hard.
* **📅 Daily challenge**: the same 10 questions for everyone that day, with a day streak.
* **👥 Two-player duel**: pass the phone.
* **Scoring**:
  * 20 seconds per question, and faster answers score more
  * harder questions are worth more
  * 🔥 a streak bonus after 3 right in a row
* **Lifelines** once per game: 50:50, +10 seconds, Skip.
* Answers flash green or red with a sound and a vibration, there's confetti for good results, and you can review all the answers at the end.
* **Challenge a friend** by sharing your score. **Stats**: games played, % right, best score, best streak.
